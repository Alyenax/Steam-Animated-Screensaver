# Demo soundtrack — Quiet Scenes

A quiet instrumental written and synthesized specifically for this presentation: warm sustained chords and sparse electric-piano tones, without vocals or drums. No third-party recordings, samples, loops, melodies or impulse responses were used.

The composition and generated recording are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), to the extent applicable rights exist. You may reuse, edit and redistribute the soundtrack, including commercially, without attribution or royalties. This dedication covers only the soundtrack, not the game artwork shown in the video.

- Reproduce: `node scripts/create-demo-music.cjs`
- Original stereo WAV: `dist/public-launch-v2/Quiet-Scenes-original.wav`
- Demo mix: quiet background level, with an opening fade and a longer closing fade.

The synthesis script remains available with the project's original code. No external music service or account is needed.
